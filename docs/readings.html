How to use this list

The lecture slides and lab notebooks are the main reading for this course — everything you are examined on is in them. The items below are optional supplementary reading: textbook chapters and published articles for the days you want to go deeper. Nothing here is required.

Each day lists textbook chapters (with the chapter title, so you can find them in any edition) and articles (the original papers and applied social-science work). Pick the textbook that suits you — you do not need all of them. A free, runnable video/tutorial companion for each day is collected in the appendix and is not part of this reading list.

If you want a single book to follow

We don’t teach from a textbook, but if you want one spine alongside the slides:

  • Concepts (free): Prince, Understanding Deep Learning — the closest match to this course, and the only one covering our whole arc (incl. variational autoencoders, diffusion, and ethics).
  • Code (the recommended text): Raff, Inside Deep Learning — PyTorch-first, light on maths.
  • Runnable chapters: Zhang et al., Dive into Deep Learning — every chapter is executable PyTorch; closest to the labs.

Note on the recommended texts. Raff is the best day-to-day companion but does not cover variational autoencoders, diffusion, or word embeddings (Days 7–8) — use Prince there. Burkov is an ML-engineering / lifecycle book; it fits Day 5 (training and evaluation discipline) and Day 12 (deployment, monitoring), not the architecture days.
